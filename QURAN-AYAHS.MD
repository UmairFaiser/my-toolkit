## 1. Surah At-Tahrim, 66:6:

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا قُوا أَنفُسَكُمْ وَأَهْلِيكُمْ نَارًا

> *“O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones…”*

Allah (SWT) is saying "O you who have beleived" allah is pointing out to his beleivers, "قُوا أَنفُسَكُمْ" protect yourself and "وَأَهْلِيكُمْ" your family members, from what? "نَارًا" from the hellfire. Initially allah is pointing out to their own selves as protect yourself from the blazing fire. Which gives every muslim their own responsibility to protect themselves from the hellfire which uses humans and jinns as firewood. And allah doesn't stop by saying to protect yourself, allah also continues to say وَأَهْلِيكُمْ and protect your family too. For those who have iman this comes in as a major responsibility. Protecting yourself and your family from the hellfire.
