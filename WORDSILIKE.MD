> for people who ask questions, "well", stupid questions.

### Impede

> means to slow down, obstruct, or make something more difficult to happen or progress.

### Defies

> refuses to obey or accept something, or goes against something.

### Decipher

> to figure out or understand something that is difficult to read, understand, or interpret.

### Solace

> comfort or relief from sadness, pain, or worry.

### Seldom

> Seldom means rarely or not often.

### deploring

> Deploring means strongly disapproving of something or expressing sadness about something bad that happened.

### Censure

> Censure means criticism or disapproval, often in a formal or serious way

### Memoirs

> Memoirs means a written account of someone's memories and experiences, usually about important or interesting parts of their life.

### Profound

> Profound means very deep, important, or having a strong effect.

### Reprimanded

> Reprimanded means officially or seriously scolded someone for doing something wrong.

### Perception

> Perception means the way you see, understand, or interpret something.

### Superficial

> Superficial means only concerned with the surface or outside of something, without going deeper.

### Serendipitous

> Serendipitous means something good or interesting that happens unexpectedly or by chance.
